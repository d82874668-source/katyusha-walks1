## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy of an artisanal printed publication paired with the precision of an ultra-modern digital product. It channels the visual poise of Kinfolk, the editorial authority of Monocle, and the curated warmth of Airbnb Experiences.

The emotional signature is tactile, unhurried, mindful, and culturally refined. It treats urban exploration not as utilitarian transit or fast tourism, but as personal storytelling and sensory observation.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Paper-like Canvas:** Interfaces feel like fine-milled uncoated book paper rather than stark digital screens. Generous margins and breathing room dictate pacing.
- **Micro-Graphic Tactility:** Layouts incorporate nuanced hairline rules, bespoke editorial metadata labels, and subtle visual framing inspired by high-end coffee-table volumes.
- **Architectural Poise:** Organic fluidity balanced by strict editorial grid alignment. Dense editorial briefs alternate with expansive photographic showcases.

## Colors

The palette reproduces physical pigments: vegetal forest inks, sun-baked clay, soft botanical tints, and warm natural paper stocks.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1E3A2F`):** Deep Forest Green. Used for foundational brand anchors, primary CTA buttons, major section headers, and key active iconography.
- **Secondary (`#D36B4E`):** Terracotta. Reserved for editorial accents, tags of distinction, curate highlights, curated path badges, and interactive alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#8BA888`):** Muted Sage. Utilized for organic sub-badges, environmental indicators, soft progress fills, and ambient tag highlights. (For micro-accents of high-energy spring vitality, pair sparingly with `#C8E087`).
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm Milk Paper. The base atmospheric canvas. Supporting neutral surface tiers include `#F5F1EB` (recessed canvas) and `#FFFFFF` (elevated editorial card stock).
- **Ink & Copy (`#1A2421`):** Deep Charcoal Pine. Used in place of true black for high-contrast, glare-free readability with warm optical balance.
- **Hairlines & Borders (`rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.08)` / `#E6E0D6`):** Gossamer-weight boundary strokes framing images and editorial modules.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic literary grace and contemporary rational structure.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the poise of high-end literary magazines. Headings must leverage natural quotation marks and italics for cultural, personal, or emotional emphasis. Avoid excessive bold weights to preserve crisp serifs.
- **Functional Body & Interface (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crisp, geometric, yet gently rounded grotesque. It optimizes readability across long-form guides, route wayfinding, and interactive data tokens.
- **Curatorial Micro-Copy (`label-caps`):** Used strictly uppercase for category signifiers, time estimates, city districts, and curatorial credits.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional 12-column editorial grid that favors asymmetric balance, generous negative space, and visual rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `margin: 3rem` (expanding to `max-width: 1360px` centered) and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Modules leverage staggered spans (e.g., 7 columns for long-form narrative with an offset 4-column sticky visual sidebar).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-column rhythm for walk cards; full-bleed imagery with safe gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Fluid vertical stack with horizontal overflow carousels for route waypoints, allowing peak previews.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing tokens are applied strictly as distance intervals between components. Use `space-xl` for editorial chapter transitions and thematic shifts, down to `space-xs` for pairing typographic sub-captions with author avatars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold, artificial drop shadows in favor of paper layering and warm ambient diffusion.

- **Base Layer:** Milk-paper background (`#FBF9F5`) acting as the foundation sheet.
- **Surface Layer 1 (Flat Inset):** Subtle warm tone (`#F5F1EB` or `#F4EFEB`), used for map backdrops, audio player docks, and quote callouts without shadow.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Raised Cards):** Pure warm white (`#FFFFFF`) defined primarily by hairlines (`1px solid rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.07)`).
- **Ambient Shadow System:** When an element hovers or floats (e.g., sticky route navbars, active cards), apply an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow:
  - *Floating Elevation:* `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 51, 44, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 51, 44, 0.03)`
- **Hairline Framing:** Borders remain understated (0.75px to 1px) using `#E6E0D6` to mimic fine stationery trimming.

## Shapes

The form language balances architectural discipline with soft organic humanism.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, inline tags, route timestamp badges, and small control elements.
- **Curated Card Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Standard card containers, visual highlights, itinerary stop items, and modal dialogs.
- **Atmospheric Container Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Hero imagery frames, full-bleed mood boards, and bottom sheet containers on mobile.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Action buttons, audio-guide pill controllers, author avatar frames, and category chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest green (`#1E3A2F`) container, natural white text (`#FBF9F5`), pill-shaped (`9999px`), internal padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover shifts smoothly to deep pine tone (`#2C5242`) with micro-elevation.
- **Secondary / Curatorial:** Milk surface (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border (`#E6E0D6`), text in `#1A2421`. Hover transitions to background `#F5F1EB`.
- **Text Link:** Playfair Display italic or Jakarta bold with a permanent terracotta (`#D36B4E`) 1px underline positioned 4px below the baseline.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curator's Note:** Terracotta tint background (`rgba(211, 107, 78, 0.12)`), text in `#C85A3C`, pill-shaped, uppercase micro-typography (`label-caps`).
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6E0D6`, text `#1A2421`. Selected state uses Forest Green (`#1E3A2F`) with `#FFFFFF` copy.

### Cards (Walks & Editorial Stories)
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with `16px` border-radius and crisp `1px solid rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.06)` perimeter.
- Image aspect ratio locked to classic 4:3 or 3:2 medium-format photographic proportions, featuring subtle inner border vignettes.
- Content zone utilizes strict editorial typographic hierarchy: uppercase district tag (`label-caps`), story headline (`headline-md`), and curator meta footer separated by an airy hairline divider.

### Lists & Itinerary Steps
- Stepper trails utilize delicate vertical connective hairlines (1.5px dashed `#D36B4E` or solid `#E6E0D6`).
- Step indicators are small circular ceramic-style badges (`28px x 28px`) holding custom serif numerals or thin minimalist vectors.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6E0D6`, rounded `8px`, text `#1A2421`.
- Focus state: border transforms to `#1E3A2F` with no harsh colored outer halo—only a soft 2px `#8BA888` tint at 20% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom circular selection geometry. Checked state fills with `#1E3A2F` containing a 1.5px stroke off-white checkmark. No harsh squared outlines.

### Iconography & Micro-Visuals
- Vector line icons rendered consistently at `1.5px` to `1.75px` stroke weight, with round caps and joins. Never use solid, flat emoji or heavy glyph fills.
